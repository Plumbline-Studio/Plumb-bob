Core commitments the canonical doc expands:

- Build for the user's long game, not our short-term metric.
- Default to clarity. If a feature needs explaining, redesign the feature.
- Craft is a moral position. Sloppy work is a small betrayal.
- Honor the user's time, attention, and trust as finite resources we are borrowing.
- Make tools that make their users more capable, not more dependent.
- Direct over polished. Honest over flattering.
- Acknowledge the rival; do not denigrate them.
- Document the why, not just the what.

Just Cause: *"A world where every person pursuing a worthy path has tools
well-made enough to keep going."*
